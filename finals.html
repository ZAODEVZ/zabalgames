Season 1 Finals - how it actually ends.

Two finalists per track advance. Six people finish the season: two artists, two builders, two creators. There is no prediction market and no weekly-task month - both were earlier plans and neither is running. The write-up further down this page is kept only as a record of what was considered.

Each track gets its own finals, run in the last week of August. The six finalists pick their own dates inside that week - you are not being handed a slot, you are agreeing one with the people you are finishing alongside.

The builder track already has its field: Brandon, Branth and jdwalka have all submitted and finished. Artist and creator are still open. If you have been building quietly, those tracks are where two spots each are genuinely unclaimed.

The prize is the 500 USDC pool, split between the six finalists. Nothing else is promised.

The shortlist, forming

Kept from an earlier plan in which mentors shortlisted July builds. That is not how Season 1 ends - Zaal picks the six finalists himself and there is no vote. Anything listed below is a leftover mentor pick, not a decision.

Why a market, not a vote - SUPERSEDED

The original plan was a one-person-one-vote Respect ballot. Clean but abstract - a click is a low-commitment signal. A wallet putting money on a build is a different commitment entirely, and the market price aggregates a lot more information than a poll.

Switching to WaveWarZ-Base gives the Finals four things at once: a real economic signal for "would I actually use this build", mass participation (anyone with a Base wallet can trade, not just Respect-holders), built-in revenue for builders (1% trade cut flows forever), and a real launch event for the WaveWarZ-Base agentic build Sam is shipping.

Full architectural decision in Doc 720. Cross-linked from Doc 701 Part 12.

How the Finals run

NOW - THE BOARD IS OPEN

Everyone who entered is on it

All 15 entrants are on the board at /leaderboard, in the track they entered. Points come from showing up: the zm stream at 5pm EST, the weekly challenge, shipping an update.

SUNDAY, AUGUST 16 - THE BOARD CLOSES

Six get picked

Zaal picks six finalists, two from each track. It is a decision, not a tally - the board is one of the things he reads, not the thing that decides.

THE SIX AGREE THE DATES

Three nights, last week of August

The six finalists agree the dates between themselves rather than being handed a slot. Three separate finals, one per track.

FINALS NIGHT - EACH TRACK

Two finalists present

The two finalists in that track present their work live. Mentors are in the room. Anyone can watch.

THE RESULT

Three winners, one per track

One winner comes out of each track - artist, builder and creator. Three winners in total across the three nights.

THE PRIZE

500 USDC, split six ways

Every finalist receives an equal share of the 500 USDC pool, sponsored by the ZAO festivals team. The same for all six, not a slice by rank.

AFTER

The work stays yours

You keep the repo and everything you built, and the 1% WaveWarZ trade cut keeps paying after August.

What Respect is (and is not)

Respect is The ZAO's governance signal. Soulbound, illiquid, non-transferable - you cannot sell it or trade it. It is what you earn by participating in the ecosystem.

During ZABAL Gamez: up to 1000 Respect points are earnable per active participant. Current ZAO holders determine who counts as active (workshop attendance, July submissions, August Finals participation, ecosystem contribution beyond just showing up). Respect maps to governance weight, not cash.

Voter eligibility: 1000+ Respect points = eligible to vote in Finals settlement. This threshold may shift as the event unfolds. The 1000+ cohort also serves as the meta-governance body - they can vote (66%+ majority) to change the governance system itself.

How Respect plays into the Finals: Respect-holders have a voice in settlement. The specific weight mechanism (how Respect interacts with the WaveWarZ-Base market) is being designed alongside the protocol and will be locked before the August Finals.

Builders get more than the prize pool

  • An equal share of the $500 USDC pool for every finalist. Confirmed before the demo night.
  • 1% of all trade volume on your battle entry, forever. Per WaveWarZ protocol. The battle entry stays live post-Finals; new trades keep flowing 1% to you.
  • Finalists present their own projects on the night. The 1% WaveWarZ trade cut keeps paying after August.
  • Respect earned during the event itself.
  • Distribution amplification from ZAO accounts across Farcaster, X, Telegram, newsletter.
  • The open-source repo + the 24h live-coding footage as content assets.
