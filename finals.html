August - The Finals

The July builds run it out.

The August Finals are a curated build month on loops.house. Everyone who submitted a July open build auto-qualifies. Two weeks of weekly tasks per track, then the top 2 in each track face off in WaveWarZ battles - one winner per track. A $500 USDC pool, tiered so every finalist who ships gets paid, plus a collectible for every finisher.

How the Finals work

Everyone who submitted a July open build auto-qualifies - no separate application, the July submission is the entry. Three tracks run in parallel: artist, builder, creator.

WEEKS 1-2

Weekly tasks, per track

Each week, each track gets a new task to build and share out publicly. Two weeks, two tasks per track. Week 1 is final-touches week - projects are posted publicly and the community gives feedback right on the posts, so builders sharpen from real feedback.

FINALE

WaveWarZ battles

After the two weeks, the top 2 in each track advance into 3-5 WaveWarZ battles - the ZAO live head-to-head battle format - to crown one winner per track: an artist winner, a builder winner, a creator winner.

Full format, dates, and the loops.house page: /content.

Prizes

A $500 USDC pool sponsored by The ZAO festivals team, tiered so every finalist who ships gets paid, plus $ZABAL token rewards. A commemorative collectible for every finisher, and a Champion collectible for each track winner. Specific per-finalist allocation is confirmed before winners lock.

Prize pool sponsor: The ZAO festivals team

The $500 USDC v0 (Season 1) prize pool is donated by the ZAO festivals team. Looking for more sponsors to grow the pool. Reach: info@thezao.com
