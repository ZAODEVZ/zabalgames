How the finals work

Now - this week

Climb the leaderboard

The field is every July submitter. Zaal picks the six finalists - there is no vote. If you submitted, you are in the running - this week is your last chance to move up.

The cut

Top 6 advance

When the week closes, the top 2 people per track - artist, builder, creator - advance. Finalists are people, not projects: your whole body of July work carries you.

End of August

The finals

All six finalists present their work live at the end of the month. One winner per track.

Three mentors, one per track

Each track gets a ZAO mentor for the month: guidance for the whole track while the leaderboard is open, then direct 1:1 prep with their two finalists for the end-of-month presentation. Mentors are named here as each one confirms.
