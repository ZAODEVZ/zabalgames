One winner per track. The artist and creator battles run inside a one-hour Space; the builder battle runs for 24 hours. Each battle's timing is on its row below. Results land on the results page.

How the 500 USDC splits

Every finalist is paid. Winning pays more than losing, and drawing a crowd pays on top of both.

  • 100 USDC to each track champion. Three champions, one per track.
  • 50 USDC to each runner-up. Nobody who reached a final leaves with nothing.
  • 50 USDC to whichever of the six draws the highest trading volume across the whole season, decided after all three battles.

That is 300 to the champions, 150 to the runners-up and 50 for volume: the full 500.

Judging is open

Every battle is decided by three signals and two of three wins: an open poll anyone can vote in with no wallet, the charts from live trading on the battle, and a judges panel. No single signal decides it, so a judge is never carrying the call alone.

A seat is one battle, about an hour, live. You score the work. No crypto experience needed. If you want one, email info@thezao.com or say so in the /zabal group.

Season 2

Season 1 was three months, open to anyone, any tool. If you want in on the next one, email info@thezao.com and say which of these you are. No form, no application - one line is enough.

  • Contestant - you want to build and compete.
  • Presenter - you want to run a session on what you know.
  • Judge - you want to score a battle.
  • Supporter - you want to back the prize pool or help it run.
