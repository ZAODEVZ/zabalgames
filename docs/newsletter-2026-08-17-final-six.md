## How the finals work

Head-to-head battles on WaveWarZ, live-traded music battles. Champions are decided by the market plus a judges panel of the 3 track mentors, with one more scoring factor locking in on Thursday.

Prizes per track: 300 USDC on the head-to-head split 70/30, plus 200 USDC volume-weighted across the battles, capped at 80.

The finals run the last week of August. Exact dates get set by the six.
