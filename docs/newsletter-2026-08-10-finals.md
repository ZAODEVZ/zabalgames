Good morning. This is the one that matters, so it is longer than usual and worth
the read.

six of you agree the dates between yourselves rather than being handed a slot.

newsletter. Nothing is closed, nothing is decided, and there is a real way to move

## The six are picked, not tallied

I am choosing the finalists at the end of this week.

The vote at zabalgamez.com/vote was built to help me curate the open submissions,
and that is exactly what it is doing. It is one of the loudest things I am reading
and it has genuinely changed how I see this season. But it is an input to the
decision, not the decision, and I would rather you hear that from me now than work
it out from a list later.

Here is how the vote itself works, because it is a good system and it deserves the
explanation. Everyone gets 100 credits per track. Backing one person costs the
square of the votes you give them, so one vote costs 1, two costs 4, three costs
9, and ten votes costs the entire 100. That means no single account can buy a
track, and spread support counts for more than piling on. One ballot per Farcaster
account. Your ballot stays private, only the totals are public, and you can change
it any time before it closes.

## The bonus: how to move this week

New, and it starts now. There is one bonus brief, it is open to everyone, and
answering it moves you up before I pick.

**The brief: design the next way fans fund an event and share in it.**

Pick a brand to build it for - ZAO Festivals, WaveWarZ, or ZABAL Gamez.

Here is the real problem with real numbers. ZAOstock is October 3. It costs 5 to 7
thousand to put on and projects 8.7 to 10.2 thousand back. Tickets are the biggest
single line at 4.7 thousand. Right now it runs on grants, sponsors and ticket
sales, all of it one directional. Money goes in, the day happens, and the people
who showed up own none of what they helped make.

Three things make this hard, and interesting:

1. The event ends. It is one day. Whatever someone holds has to still mean
   something on October 4th.
2. Tickets already work. If your model does not wrap or beat the thing people
   already pay for, it is just a second ask.
3. It is 6 thousand, not 6 million. This has to work at community scale.

What already exists, so you build instead of rebuild: Sparkz is a launcher with
multi-recipient fee splits and it is running. Ticketing is decided, Unlock on Base
plus POAP. Treasury tools were evaluated and not adopted.

What does not exist, and is where the space is: fans co-owning anything. Anything
that carries across more than one event. Rewarding people after an event rather
than before it.

**Answer it in any format.** A document, a diagram, a contract, a working demo, a
five minute walkthrough, a video, a track if you can pull it off. We care about the
thinking, not the polish.

**Post it in the /zabal channel on Farcaster.** That is the whole entry mechanic.
If it is not in /zabal it does not count.

A bonus answer moves you up. If you are sitting outside the top two in your track
right now, this is the fastest way to change that before I pick at the end of the
week. If you are already at the top, it is how you stay there.

## What the six are playing for

The prize pool is $500 USDC, funded by The ZAO festivals team, and it is split
across the six so that everybody who makes it gets paid.

**$300 on the battles.** Three WaveWarZ community battles, one per track, the two
finalists in that track facing each other. Win yours and you take $70. Lose it and
you take $30. There is no zero.

**$200 on volume.** Split across all six according to how much trading volume your
battle pulls in, capped at $80 per person so that one large trade cannot swallow
the pool.

The most any one person can walk away with is $150.

On top of the pool, straight from WaveWarZ itself: **1% of every trade on your side
of the battle, forever.** The battle entry stays live after the finals, and new
trades keep paying the artist. That is the WaveWarZ model, built by Hurric4n3Ike,
and it is why the finals run there.

Plus both collectibles. Everyone who finishes gets the finisher. The champions get
the champion. The June prize tier stands in full.
