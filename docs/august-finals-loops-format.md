# ZABAL Gamez - August Finals format (loops.house + WaveWarZ)

Status: PROPOSED (pending RK / loops.house reply). Dates TBD - none locked.
Owner: Zaal. Last updated: 2026-07-26.

This is the canonical spec for the August "curated build month" - the ZABAL Gamez
Finals. It replaces the older single-shot Finals sketch in `CLAUDE.md` (24h build / 24h
promote / 24h vote / reveal) with a 2-week curated-task format capped by WaveWarZ battles,
hosted on loops.house.

## The format

**Who is in:** anyone who submitted an open build during July's open build month
auto-qualifies. No separate application - the July submission IS the entry. (Open builds
live at zabalgamez.com/submissions; the vote is at /vote.)

**Structure - 2 weeks of weekly tasks:**
- Three tracks run in parallel: **artist** (musical/visual), **builder** (developer),
  **creator** (media/distribution).
- Each week, each track gets a NEW task to build/make and **share out publicly**.
- Two weeks = two tasks per track. Build + share is the unit of progress.

**Finale - WaveWarZ battles:**
- After the 2 weeks, the **top 2 in each track** advance.
- They face off in **3-5 WaveWarZ battles** (the ZAO live head-to-head battle format).
- Outcome: **one winner per track** - an artist winner, a builder winner, a creator winner.

**Virtual:** fully online.

## Prizes

- **$500 USDC pool** (sponsored by The ZAO festivals team), tiered - every finalist who
  ships gets paid.
- A commemorative **collectible** for every finisher (via Magnetiq).
- Looking for more sponsors to grow the pool (open to loops.house / partner sponsorship).

## loops.house integration

loops.house is the hosting/leaderboard surface. Their native format is **Build Battles**
(biweekly, 10-hour ship windows, AI judging, a unified rating ladder from Builder 0-999 to
Grandmaster 2200+). A loops hackathon page carries: hero + title, a stats bar
(registered / submissions / prize pool / leaderboard), About, Sponsors + Prizes, and an FAQ
(eligibility, team size, submission = title + pitch + description + repo/demo).

**Fit note:** our format (weekly tasks over 2 weeks + a WaveWarZ finale, community-judged)
differs from their default 10-hour build battle. The open questions to RK decide the bridge.
