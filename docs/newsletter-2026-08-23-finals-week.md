## How a battle is decided

Three signals. Two of three wins.

1. **The open poll.** Anyone can vote. No wallet needed.
2. **The charts.** Live trading volume on the battle.
3. **The judges.** Independent scoring of the work itself.

Why three and not one: a poll on its own is a popularity contest. A market on its own belongs to whoever has the most money. Judges on their own are one room deciding for everyone. Each covers the others' blind spot, and no single one can hand somebody a win.

## Where the battles run

We are running them on WaveWarZ. Two artists go head to head, the community decides, and the result settles on its own. It has been running since 2025 and has completed 1,289 battles. We did not build it for our finals. We are running ours on it.

Trading a battle is how you back somebody with more than a vote. You trade while it is live, and that volume is one of the three signals. The artist earns 1% of every trade on their battle, paid automatically, win or lose. Backing them pays them directly.

## How the 500 USDC splits

- **100 USDC** to each track champion
- **50 USDC** to each runner-up
- **50 USDC** to whichever of the six pulls the highest trading volume across their WaveWarZ battle

Every finalist is paid. Winning pays double. Drawing a crowd pays on top of both.

All four count. Two of them are two of the three signals.
