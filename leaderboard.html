ZABAL Gamez Season 1 Roster

The 15 who entered ZABAL Gamez Season 1.

ZABAL Gamez ran June, July and August. June was workshops, July was the open build, August is the finals. Anyone could enter, it cost nothing, and 15 people did.

There are three tracks - artist, builder and creator. Everyone who entered appears below, in every track they joined. Six became finalists, two from each track. They go head to head on WaveWarZ between August 24 and 30, and three win, one from each track.
