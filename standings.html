Qualifier standings

Weeks 1-2 are open to everyone who submitted. Post a daily update to your project to earn points, rally community votes, and climb your track. The top 2 per track advance to the battle weeks.

How the ranking works. Each project is scored on two parts, both shown below so it is transparent. Daily-update points: one point per day you post a progress update to your project (one per day, so consistency wins). Votes: the community quadratic ballot at /vote (one ballot per Farcaster account). Each part is scaled to 0-100 within its track and combined, then the top 2 per track are marked as advancing. Two different builders take the two slots per track.
