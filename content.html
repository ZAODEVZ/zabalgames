This Month at ZABAL Gamez

August - The Finals.

August is the Finals month. If you submitted anything in July - any format, partial counts - you are in. No new application. Everyone in the pool gets paired with a ZAO mentor and a pod, and builds toward the Finals together. Here is what is coming.

The August Arc

The shape of the month. Dates firm up as the week lands - watch /live for what is on right now.

Wk 1

Mentor pairing core

Every July builder is matched with an embedded ZAO mentor and placed in a pod. The pool is the July submissions board - if you submitted, you already have a seat.

Wk 2-3

Pods build

Pods work with their mentor toward a Finals-ready build. Mentors share the evaluation criteria up front, so everyone knows what "ready" looks like. Support is the whole point - no one builds alone.

Late Aug

The Finals

Finals builds go up for the community. WaveWarZ-Base integration and on-chain settlement decide it - the market, not a panel. Details on the Finals page.

The Tracks

Three ways to be in it. Pick one, or more than one - partial and work-in-progress counts everywhere.

BUILDER

Build

Ship something real on the ZAO rails - WaveWarZ, Empire Builder, Zlank, Farcaster. Adopt a started project or bring your own.

ARTIST

Create

Music, art, cover work, visuals. The ZAO is music-first - artists carry the Games as much as builders do.

CREATOR

Document

Clips, recaps, threads, build-in-public. Tell the story of what the community is shipping and you are in the pool too.

Mentor Pods

The heart of August. Instead of a leaderboard grind, every builder gets a ZAO mentor and a small pod - a handful of builders working alongside a mentor toward the Finals. Mentors pair with the open pool, share evaluation criteria, and give real feedback, not a score at the end.

Mentor roster is open. Want to mentor a pod? Sign up on the /lead form and pick "Mentor" or "Both." Confirmed mentors are added as they come in.

What's Dropping This Month

The public stuff coming across August - broadcasts, recaps, and drops. Come watch the build in public.

  • Finals broadcast - the Finals go live for the community; watch on /finals/live.
  • Pod check-ins - Spaces and streams through the month; see /spaces + /streams.
  • Session recaps - what each pod is shipping, written up as it happens: /recaps.
  • Daily update - the one-glance "what's on today": /today.

Get In

Free, open to anyone, all month:

  • Submitted in July? You are already in the pool - see the board.
  • Enter the build if you have not yet.
  • Mentor a pod (pick "Mentor" on the lead form).
  • Just join /zabal and follow along.

Finals prizes are backed by the ZAO. Sponsor a track or a prize? Reach out in /zabal.
