This Month at ZABAL Gamez

The August Finals.

The August Finals of ZABAL Gamez, The ZAO's three-month build-a-thon. Everyone who submitted an open build in July is in. Over two weeks, artists, builders, and creators each get a new weekly task to make and share. The top 2 in each track then go head-to-head in WaveWarZ battles to crown a winner per track.

Tracks

ARTIST

Musical / Visual

Music, art, cover work, visuals. The ZAO is music-first - artists carry the Games.

BUILDER

Developer / Technical

Ship something real on the ZAO rails - WaveWarZ, Empire Builder, Zlank, Farcaster.

CREATOR

Media / Distribution

Clips, recaps, threads, build-in-public. Tell the story of what the community ships.

Eligibility

Open to anyone who submitted an open build during July's open build month. If you submitted - any format, partial counts - you are in. No new application.

Format

Two weeks of weekly tasks, then a battle finale. Everything runs in August, fully online.

Week 1

Weekly task, per track

A task drops for each track. Build it, share it.

Week 2

A new weekly task, per track

A fresh task per track. Build it, share it.

Finale

WaveWarZ battles

The top 2 per track go into 3-5 WaveWarZ battles - one winner per track. Decided by the market, not a panel. See the Finals page.

Exact dates are announced soon. Watch /live for what's on right now.

Prizes

A $500 USDC pool sponsored by The ZAO festivals team, tiered so every finalist who ships gets paid - plus a commemorative collectible for every finisher.

What you submit each week

That week's task deliverable: a title, a short pitch, a description, and a link - a live URL, repo, demo, video, or track.

Partners

The ZAO festivals team (prize pool) · Empire Builder · WaveWarZ (the battle finale) · loops.house (host & platform)

Get In

  • Submitted in July? You are already in - see the board.
  • Enter the build if you have not yet.
  • Follow along in the /zabal Farcaster channel.
