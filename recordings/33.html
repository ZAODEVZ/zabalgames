POIDH is a social bounty platform. And so the idea behind it is that you can crowdfund any outcome and that you can verify that it happened and you can do it all trustlessly, 100% on chain.- Kenny (POIDH)
Email Remittance Pro... basically no matter who it is across the globe, if you have their email, you can send them crypto with just their email. The email is the identity layer.- Dr. Deeks (as heard)

Farcaster Batches Day 1, hosted on the GM Farcaster X account by Nounish Prof and Adrian, with five builders from the batch presenting. Chris Dolinsky showed Vini App, a no-code platform that turns natural-language prompts into working web3 apps, with a $1,000+ USDC prize pool running for the hackathon week. Kenny presented POIDH, the on-chain social bounty platform where any outcome can be crowdfunded and verified trustlessly, with $25 boosts for the first 20 new bounties tied to Farcaster projects. Nick (nickysap) walked through Juke, a native Farcaster audio client with voice notes, Space recording and an SDK for embedding audio in apps - The ZAO is named as an SDK user. Jonathan Colton covered FounderCheck, an agent-powered way for founders to test their go-to-market messaging, plus the Photocaster launch where artists keep 100 percent of proceeds. Dr. Deeks (as heard) closed with Email Remittance Pro, Crafter and Ghostwriter. The through-line: tools that give creators and users in the Farcaster ecosystem more leverage. Zaal is referenced but does not speak on this recording.

Topics
Farcaster Batches no-code web3 apps on-chain bounties POIDH Juke founder validation artist sovereignty crypto remittance
Key takeaways
  • Vini App lets non-coders generate a working web3 app from natural language in minutes.
  • POIDH runs on ephemeral one-task DAOs: crowdfund an outcome, verify it, settle it on-chain - the $30k kickflip world record is the reference case.
  • A custom Farcaster client like Juke still matters after native Spaces shipped, because it can add voice notes, recording and an SDK faster than the protocol does.
  • FounderCheck measured go-to-market clarity improving 3.3+ points in a single session.
  • Photocaster keeps 100 percent of proceeds with the artist, positioned against the older Rodeo model.
  • Email Remittance Pro uses an email address as the identity layer to send crypto across chains without a wallet setup.
