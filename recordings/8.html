Earn from music battles, not streams: WaveWarz

Hurricane Ike · @hurric4n3ike · WaveWarz · Artist track - Recorded workshop

Technical3/10Approachable
Watch on YouTube Read the transcript WaveWarz

Hurricane Ike, founder of WaveWarz, on a new way for musicians to get paid: head-to-head music battles where people buy and sell on the competing tracks like stocks, and the artist earns one percent of every trade on their side - engagement, not streams. One artist earned about 0.5 SOL in minutes of a single battle. He covers the live chart scoring that makes a battle as fun to watch as it is to hear, the nightly 8:30pm EST quick-battle queue, and community battles in any format (music, roast, cooking) with automated payouts straight to artist wallets. Artists keep their masters, IP, and audience. 500+ SOL in volume, 40+ artists, built on Solana.

Key takeaways
  • Artists earn from engagement - one percent of every trade on their side of a battle - not from streams.
  • The live charts make a battle a game: the score moves as people buy and sell.
  • You keep your masters, IP, and audience. WaveWarz never holds them.
  • Quick battles run nightly at 8:30pm EST; anyone can queue one for 0.05 SOL.
  • Community battles run any format - music, roast, cooking - with automated, split payouts.
  • 500+ SOL in volume, 40+ artists, built on Solana for fast, low-fee trades.
